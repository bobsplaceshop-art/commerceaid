## Session in the larger arc

008K is complete. The whole stage was five sub-stages
that built the merchant-facing half of the platform:

  008K.1  Admin Orders
  008K.2  Admin Customers
  008K.3  Admin Users
  008K.4  Admin Roles
  008K.5  Order Pricing Engine

Before 008K, five sidebar items had been placeholders
since Stage 005. After 008K, every one of them is a
real admin surface. The platform can now be operated
end to end by a merchant who is not the developer.

The pricing engine (008K.5) was the last piece. It
was also the largest - three sessions plus a fix arc.
Its subtleties were the inclusive/exclusive tax
distinction (which produced the arc's P0 bug), the
per-product override (merchant-facing feature), and
the coupling of coupons, tax, and shipping into one
totals formula. The coupling principle said ship them
together. They shipped together, and the last five
commits were the practical payoff of doing so.
