The security model held through every test:

  - Public subscribe refuses to re-open an unsubscribed
    address. A third party cannot undo a subscriber's
    opt-out through the footer form.
  - The authenticated account preferences toggle calls
    `resubscribe()`, which is a deliberate re-open for
    the customer's own account.
  - Suppressed addresses are refused by both paths.

German compliance is satisfied:

  - Double opt-in by default for German deployments.
  - Consent evidence captured (source, IP, user agent,
    timestamp).
  - Unsubscribe link in every campaign email, and an
    acknowledgement email on every unsubscribe.
  - No data leaves the store's infrastructure.
